Mini Games

Fourteen small games you can play in the browser, on your phone or your laptop.

Arcade

Reflexes and reaction. Short rounds, chasing a high score.

  • 🐦 Flappy Bird Tap to flap. Don't hit the pipes.
  • 🐍 Snake Eat, grow, don't bite yourself.
  • 🧱 Breakout Bounce the ball, clear the wall.
  • 🚀 Asteroids Shoot the rocks, don't get hit.
  • 🦘 Doodle Jump Bounce up, don't fall off.

Puzzle

Shapes, numbers and deduction. Some against the clock, some not.

  • 🟪 Tetris Stack the blocks, clear the lines.
  • 🔢 2048 Slide tiles, merge to 2048.
  • 💣 Minesweeper Clear the grid, flag the mines.
  • 🧩 Sudoku Fill the grid, one to nine.

Strategy

Plan ahead and outlast whatever is coming at you.

  • 🔴 Connect Four Four in a row beats the CPU.
  • 🏰 Tower Defense Build turrets, hold the line.

Cards & words

Played at your own pace, a round at a time.

  • 🃏 Solitaire Klondike patience, one card at a time.
  • 🔤 Word Guess Six tries at a five-letter word.

Sports

Line up the shot and judge the strength.

  • ⛳ Mini Golf Nine holes, drag to putt.
